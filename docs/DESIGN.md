# Design and content decisions

Kind: an educational interactive keynote with a local software prototype. It is not a company landing page.

The user supplies the palette, three type families, section order, cinematic entrances, recurring SVG motif, Lenis and Vanta. These explicit requirements override Taste Skill’s random palette/hero dealers, two-family limit, ban on Lenis, company sales copy patterns and photo requirements. The implementation applies its equal content edges, restrained single accent, one visual system, live functional product UI, transform/opacity motion, readable controls, real-browser inspection and no invented proof.

Voice: a practical CA professional speaking to Indian CA students. Conversational explanations; exact operational terms; no fabricated client names, approvals, achievements or savings. The user’s wording supplies the signature statements.

28 scenes across nine chapters. Scene allocations total exactly 60 minutes. Every scene has a speaking script, takeaway, transition and next-scene preview in a separate presenter window. The audience DOM contains no speaking notes.

Display: locally bundled Instrument Serif regular/italic. Body: locally bundled Inter regular/medium. Technical: locally bundled IBM Plex Mono regular. Champagne is reserved for the transformation line, editorial emphasis and selected states; warm paper distinguishes explanation and product use from cinematic chapter statements.

The transformation line begins as a single task, branches in the story and opportunity sections, becomes a controlled connected path in workflow and enterprise sections, then resolves into one line at the close. Custom SVG paths are drawn by GSAP. Lenis exclusively owns smooth scrolling; ScrollTrigger observes scene boundaries; Vanta is lazy loaded and destroyed on scene changes. Only one Vanta effect is active at a time.

No photographs or third-party logos are necessary. The four presenter project slots accept local authorised screenshots; blank impact fields are intentional and required by the brief. Image object URLs and notes remain in memory, not uploaded or retained after a page reload. Synthetic expense GSTIN fields deliberately use visibly fictional identifiers.

Core scripts, fonts and dependencies are local after build. No external services, model connection or API key is used.
